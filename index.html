The useful details

Before you book.

Golf simulator and event hire across Newcastle, Gateshead, Northumberland, Sunderland, Durham and Teesside. Further afield? Tell us where you're planning your event.

Do guests need to have played golf before?

Not at all. Beginners and regular golfers are welcome. We help guests get started and adapt the format to your event.

Can the golf simulator go outdoors?

Outdoor setups may be possible, depending on the venue, cover and conditions. Share your plans with us so we can confirm a suitable arrangement.

How long does setup take?

The golf simulator is typically ready in under an hour. We'll agree arrival and setup times with you before the event.

How many people can play?

As a guide, closest-to-the-pin games suit roughly 8–12 players per hour. We can recommend a format around your guest numbers and booking duration.

Can I hire just the inflatable nightclub?

Yes. The inflatable nightclub is available separately from the golf simulator. Ask us about availability, pricing and what's included for your event.
